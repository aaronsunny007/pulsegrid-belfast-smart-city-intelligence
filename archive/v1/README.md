# 📌 Overview

PulseGrid Belfast is a cloud-native Smart City Operational Intelligence platform designed to monitor, process, and analyze transport operational data using modern cloud data engineering and analytics technologies.

The platform transforms raw transport datasets into actionable operational intelligence through scalable ETL pipelines, medallion architecture processing, Spark-based analytics, and executive-level dashboard reporting.

The solution simulates a real-world transport operations command center capable of identifying operational degradation, monitoring transport reliability, classifying service risk levels, and generating executive KPIs for infrastructure decision-making.

# 🧠 Operational Intelligence Logic

The platform classifies transport lines into operational risk categories based on reliability scoring:

| Reliability Score | Risk Classification |
| ----------------- | ------------------- |
| 90+               | Stable              |
| 80–89             | Moderate Risk       |
| Below 80          | High Risk           |

This enables rapid identification of underperforming transport services and operational degradation patterns.
